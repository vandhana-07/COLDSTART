COLDSTART // Mission Operations Primer

1. Operational Problem Statement

High-altitude stratospheric payloads reach 26 km altitude where ambient temperatures plummet to −52 °C. In the 13-hour stratospheric night, Li-ion batteries suffer massive usable capacity loss and freeze. If the battery dies, all payload avionics and sensor payloads are lost.

2. The Heater Paradox (Why the Default Fails)

With 10 mm insulation, heat leaks into the cold void. A 5 W electric heater engages to prevent freezing, but the heater itself drains the battery to 0% at T+377 min (00:14), killing the mission halfway through the night.

3. The Engineering Breakthrough: Insulation Beats Extra Cells

★ Smart Thermal Conservation Wins:
• The Lazy Fix: 10 cells + 10 mm insulation costs &inr;2,584 and survives with a razor-thin 1.10× margin.
• The Smart Fix: 8 cells + 25 mm XPS insulation costs &inr;2,210 (&inr;374 cheaper!) and delivers a robust 1.81× safety margin because energy is trapped rather than consumed on heating.
