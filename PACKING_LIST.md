# 2. YOUR PROJECT NEEDS ALMOST NOTHING (that's the point)

Our solution is software. No soldering, no wiring, no hardware dependencies.

| ✓ | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Python 3 installed and tested** on every laptop | Run `python3 server.py` at home before you leave |
| ☐ | **Offline copy of the project** on a USB stick | Venue wifi will fail. This is your insurance. |
| ☐ | **Phone with data + hotspot ON** | Your app calls live APIs. Hotspot is the backup network. |
| ☐ | **Power bank for phone** | Phone dies = hotspot dies = demo dies |
| ☐ | **Extension board / multi-plug** | Power points are always scarce. Bring **one**, not three. |
| ☐ | Demo video recorded on your phone | Pre-recorded 3-min run, in case everything else breaks |

> Test this before you arrive: **turn off wifi, keep mobile data on, run the app.** The app
> falls back to cached real data and labels it `CACHED` — that behaviour is your safety net.
> Verify it actually works.

# 4. OVERNIGHT COMFORT — it's a 24-hour event, 02:00 is the hard part

Chennai is hot outside, but **AC halls get genuinely cold between 1 AM and 5 AM.** Most
first-timers pack wrong.

| ✓ | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Light hoodie / blanket / shawl** | The #1 thing people forget. AC halls at 3 AM. |
| ☐ | Toothbrush + toothpaste + face wash | You'll want it at 6 AM |
| ☐ | **Eye mask + earplugs** | Sleeping in shifts in a loud hall |
| ☐ | Reusable water bottle | Stay hydrated; refill stations usually available |
| ☐ | Comfortable footwear / flip-flops | 24 hours on your feet |
| ☐ | **Change of clothes** | Chennai humidity — you'll want a fresh t-shirt |
| ☐ | **Small meds kit:** paracetamol, ORS, band-aids, antacid, **your personal meds** | Venue first-aid may be limited |
| ☐ | Tissues / wet wipes, sanitiser | |
| ☐ | Caffeine or snacks you actually like | **Food and refreshments ARE provided (FAQ 05)** — so only bring your own preferences |

**Not needed:** food, water supply, drinks. FAQ 05 confirms food and refreshments are provided
for the full 24 hours.
